About Me

Trained as an engineer. Working like one, when it helps the design.

I started in Computer Science & Engineering at International Islamic University Chittagong, which is where the habit came from: before I open Figma, I want to know what the screen is actually for, who's using it, and what happens if the design gets that wrong. Medi-DiaBioTrack, my most detailed project, exists because "just show the number" isn't good enough when the number is someone's blood sugar.

Most of my design work has come from self-directed projects and short internships rather than one long in-house role, which has pushed me to move fast across formats: a health app, an ordering flow, a brand-driven marketing site, a dashboard redesign, and one motion-led concept site. Alongside design, I've spent the last two years in leadership roles inside IEEE and IIUC's Computer Club, running events, coordinating teams, and communicating decisions to people who weren't in the room when they were made.
